## 🧭 About Me

I'm interested in understanding physical phenomena through equations and applying that understanding to real systems.
I study numerical methods for heat transfer and fluid dynamics problems, and explore how theory such as the gamma function, complex analysis, and fractional calculus can inform engineering models.

Recently, I've expanded my interests into the defense industry and security. I'm researching defense export policy and learning software and IoT vulnerability analysis step by step, coming at it from a non-CS background.
